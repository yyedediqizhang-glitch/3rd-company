Hong Kong · Social enterprise香港 · 社會企業

3D printing for a more inclusive society打印一個更共融的社會

We collect the plastic Hong Kong throws away and turn it into something useful: classroom learning, neighbourhood workshops, and low-cost aids for patients and carers.我們收集香港被丟棄的塑膠，把它變成有用的東西：課堂學習、社區工作坊，以及病人與照顧者都負擔得起的輔助器具。

Three places we work我們工作的三個場景

The same idea runs through each programme: plastic that would be thrown away becomes something a person can use.同一個想法貫穿三個計劃：原本會被丟棄的塑膠，變成真正有人用得著的東西。

Educate教育

Students learn by making — anatomy models printed in class, then volunteering to teach the next group how to sort plastic and run a printer.學生從動手做中學習：在課堂打印人體解剖模型，然後成為義工，教下一班同學分類塑膠和操作打印機。

Community社區

We place collection points for clean PET, PP and HDPE bottles in housing estates, and reward residents with supermarket vouchers.我們在屋苑設置收集點，回收潔淨的 PET、PP 及 HDPE 膠樽，並以超市禮券回饋居民。

Medical醫療

Hospitals can turn their own clean plastic waste into customised prosthetics, dental models and surgical practice models.醫院可以把自身的潔淨塑膠廢料，製成度身訂造的義肢、牙科模型及手術練習模型。

From a bottle to a helping hand從一個膠樽，到一雙援手

Six steps, all of them visible to the people taking part — nothing is shipped overseas to disappear.六個步驟，全部讓參與者親眼看見——沒有把廢料運到海外，眼不見為淨。

  1. 01

    Collect收集

    Clean PET, PP and HDPE bottles arrive at our community points and hospital collection bins.潔淨的 PET、PP、HDPE 膠樽送到社區收集點及醫院回收箱。

  2. 02

    Sort and clean分類清洗

    Volunteers check the plastic code, remove caps and labels, then wash and dry the material.義工核對塑膠編號、去除瓶蓋與標籤，再清洗及烘乾材料。

  3. 03

    Process加工

    The plastic is shredded and processed into filament that our printers can use.塑膠被粉碎並加工成打印機可用的線材。

  4. 04

    Print打印

    Workshop participants design and print something useful: a teaching model, a phone stand, a new grip for a walking stick.工作坊參加者設計並打印實用物件：教具、手機座、拐杖的新握把。

  5. 05

    Give back回饋

    The finished object goes to a student, a resident or a patient — and the skills stay in the community.完成品送到學生、居民或病人手上，而技術就留在社區。

  6. 06

    Return回用

    Failed prints and supports go back to the shredder, so the loop closes instead of ending in a bin.打印失敗的成品與支撐材料會送回粉碎步驟，讓循環閉合，而不是丟進垃圾桶。

Technology should not be a privilege for the few. It should belong to the whole society.科技不應是少數人的特權，而應屬於整個社會。
