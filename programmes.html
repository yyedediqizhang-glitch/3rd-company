What we do我們做什麼

Our programmes我們的計劃

Three programmes, one idea: plastic that would be wasted becomes something a person can use. Each one is run with a partner who knows the people in the room.三個計劃，一個想法：原本會被浪費的塑膠，變成真正有人用得著的東西。每個計劃都與熟悉參加者的夥伴合辦。

Programme 01計劃 01

Education: learning by making教育：從動手做中學習

3D printing is an emerging educational technology. Connected to STEAM teaching, it changes the way students meet a difficult idea: they hold it.三維打印是新興的教育技術。與 STEAM 教學結合，它改變了學生面對困難概念的方式：他們可以把概念拿在手上。

What a school session includes學校工作坊內容

  • A short introduction to additive manufacturing and the circular economy三維打印與循環經濟簡介
  • Hands-on plastic sorting, and a demonstration of how filament is made親手分類塑膠，並示範線材的製作過程
  • A design task the class can keep and improve可以帶走、繼續改良的設計任務
  • A printed teaching model for the school送給學校的打印教具
  • Optional: training for students who want to volunteer at later workshops可選：培訓有意在日後工作坊擔任義工的學生

It improves the learning experience and encourages self-directed learning and innovation. Teachers use it in three ways: teaching 3D printing itself, supporting other subjects, and making learning aids. A printed organ model makes human anatomy easier to grasp than a diagram on a page.它提升學習體驗，鼓勵自主學習與創新。教師的用法有三種：直接教授三維打印、作為其他科目的支援技術，以及製作教具。一個打印出來的人體器官模型，比書頁上的圖解更容易理解。

It also changes how students learn. Instead of memorising, they explore, make and test — critical making rather than rote learning. When they are ready, they step out of the textbook and volunteer at our workshops, teaching participants to operate a printer and to sort plastic properly. Students stop being only receivers of knowledge and become people who pass it on.它也改變了學生的學習方式。他們不再只是背誦，而是探索、製作與測試——是批判性的動手做，而不是死記硬背。準備好之後，他們會走出課本，在我們的工作坊擔任義工，教參加者操作打印機、正確分類塑膠。學生成為知識的傳遞者，而不只是接收者。

Programme 02計劃 02

Community: recycling that pays back社區：回收有回報

A recycling point only works if people use it. Ours gives something back — vouchers for the plastic, and skills for the people who bring it.回收點要有人用才有意義。我們的回收點會回饋：膠樽換禮券，而帶來膠樽的人也可以學到技術。

Bring your bottles帶膠樽來 Clean, empty bottles with caps and labels removed. We accept recycling codes 1 (PET), 5 (PP) and 2 (HDPE) only.潔淨、空的膠樽，請先移除瓶蓋與標籤。我們只接受回收編號 1（PET）、5（PP）及 2（HDPE）。

We set up recycling stations in the community to collect clean PET, PP and HDPE plastic bottles. The plastic is then repurposed for 3D printing. To encourage residents to take part and to build a sustainable daily habit, we offer supermarket and shopping mall vouchers.我們在社區設置回收站，收集潔淨的 PET、PP 及 HDPE 膠樽，再將其改用作三維打印材料。為了鼓勵居民參與、逐步建立可持續的日常習慣，我們提供超市及商場禮券作為回饋。

University student volunteers run the on-site workshops. Participants learn 3D thinking, sketching and design, then operate a printer and watch waste plastic become a new object. That is how STEM knowledge reaches a local community, and how ordinary citizens get access to an emerging technology.由大學義工主持現場工作坊。參加者學習三維思維、草圖與設計，然後親自操作打印機，看著廢塑膠變成一件新物件。STEM 知識就是這樣走進社區，一般市民就是這樣接觸到新興技術。

Copyright, taught properly版權，要教得清楚

Many people have little legal awareness of what can be printed. Our workshops cover permitted use, adaptation and plagiarism, remind participants to avoid infringement, and encourage original design. The aim is a higher level of digital legal awareness — and a more cohesive community.很多人對「可以打印什麼」缺乏法律意識。工作坊會講解使用限制、改作與抄襲，提醒參加者避免侵權，同時鼓勵原創設計。目標是提升公眾的數碼法律意識，也讓社區更凝聚。

Programme 03計劃 03

Medical: made close to the patient醫療：在病人身邊製造

3D printing has shown real potential in medicine. Applying circular economy principles to it can deliver environmental benefits and improve the quality of care at the same time.三維打印在醫療領域展現出實質潛力。把循環經濟原則應用其中，可以同時帶來環境效益與更好的護理質素。

Where we stop我們的分界 We do not print anything that touches a patient’s body without the hospital’s clinical and infection-control teams. Medical work follows the partner hospital’s material, sterilisation and regulatory requirements — not our own judgement.任何會接觸病人身體的物件，都必須在醫院臨床及感染控制團隊參與下才會製作。醫療項目一律依從合作醫院的材料、消毒及法規要求，而不是我們自己的判斷。

Clinical use臨床應用

Personalised prosthetics and dental devices, made to a patient’s own measurements rather than adapted from a standard size.個人化的義肢與牙科裝置，按病人本身的尺寸製作，而不是把標準尺寸改裝。

Non-clinical use非臨床應用

Surgical models for simulation and practice, so a team can rehearse a difficult procedure before it happens to a patient.用於模擬與練習的手術模型，讓團隊可以在真正動手術之前先演練一次。

Recycling inside the hospital醫院內的循環

Hospitals can also recycle their own clean plastic waste into products through in-house printing, moving from a linear model to a sustainable circulation system. Using recycled material for assistive devices lowers the cost of medical supplies and reduces the financial burden on patients.醫院也可以把自身的潔淨塑膠廢料，透過院內打印製成產品，由線性模式走向可持續的循環系統。以回收材料製作輔助器具，可以降低醫療用品的成本，減輕病人的經濟負擔。

How we designed these programmes我們如何設計這些計劃

Before writing a single workshop plan, we worked through six questions. They still shape every programme we run.在寫第一份工作坊計劃之前，我們先回答了六條問題。它們至今仍然影響每一個計劃。

Interest興趣

For many people 3D printing is a new technology. For us the attraction is specific: eco-friendly plastic materials, and a market that is still largely untapped.對很多人來說，三維打印是新技術。對我們而言，吸引之處很具體：環保塑膠材料，以及仍然大致未被開發的市場。

Frustrations挫折

There are few successful local precedents to learn from. Hong Kong differs from other cities in its lack of promotion and public awareness of 3D printing.本地可供參考的成功先例不多。香港與其他城市不同之處，在於對三維打印的推廣與公眾認知都不足。

Problems問題

Research, material supply, social need and environmental requirements are disconnected. Medical 3D printing also faces questions of regulatory oversight and material safety.研究技術、材料供應、社會需求與環保要求彼此脫節。醫療三維打印亦面對法規監管與材料安全的問題。

Solutions方案

Combine 3D printing with the 3R principles: plastic collection points, community education workshops, and recycling inside hospitals to make medical assistive products.把三維打印與 3R 原則結合：設立塑膠收集點、舉辦社區教育工作坊，並在醫院內回收塑膠以製作醫療輔助產品。

Customers顧客

People who are under-served: students in education, community residents, and hospitals, older people and disabled people who need low-cost custom aids.被忽略的一群：教育界的學生、社區居民，以及需要低成本度身訂造輔助器具的醫院、長者與殘疾人士。

Appeal吸引力

Lower the barriers with low-cost, eco-friendly technology, reward recycling with vouchers, and empower communities through university volunteers. Tech for Good, People First.以低成本、環保的技術降低門檻，用禮券回饋回收，並透過大學義工賦能社區。科技向善，以人為本。

Which programme fits you?哪個計劃適合你？

If you are a school, start with education. If you manage a housing estate or a community centre, start with a collection point. If you work in a hospital or a clinic, start with a conversation about materials and oversight.如果你是學校，可以由教育計劃開始。如果你管理屋苑或社區中心，可以由設置收集點開始。如果你在醫院或診所工作，可以先和我們談材料與監管要求。
