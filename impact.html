Why it matters為什麼重要

Impact and what comes next成效與下一步

We count success in three ways: what people learn, what stays out of landfill, and what becomes cheaper and easier to get.我們用三種方式衡量成效：人們學到什麼、有多少東西不必送往堆填區，以及有什麼變得更便宜、更容易得到。

Who benefits誰受惠

A social enterprise has to be useful to more than one group at once. These are the five we design for.社會企業要同時對多個群體有用。以下是我們設計時考慮的五個群體。

Students學生

A way into STEM that is hands-on and creative. Older students also gain teaching experience as volunteers, which is useful on its own terms.一條動手、有創意的 STEM 入門路徑。較年長的學生更可以在義工服務中獲得教學經驗，這本身就有價值。

Residents社區居民

A reason to recycle — vouchers — and free workshops that give access to a technology they would otherwise only read about.一個回收的理由——禮券——以及免費工作坊，讓他們接觸到本來只會在報道中看見的技術。

Hospitals and patients醫院與病人

Customised aids and models at lower cost, and a route for the hospital’s own clean plastic waste to become something useful.更低成本的度身訂造輔助器具與模型，也為醫院自身的潔淨塑膠廢料找到有用出路。

Older people and disabled people長者與殘疾人士

Affordable, repairable aids. When a part wears out, a replacement can be printed instead of a whole new device being bought.負擔得起、可以維修的輔助器具。零件磨損時，可以打印替換件，而不必整件重新購買。

University volunteers大學義工

Real practice in teaching, facilitation and design — and a reason to understand the material cycle rather than just the printer.在教學、帶領活動與設計上的實際鍛鍊，也讓他們理解整個材料循環，而不只是操作打印機。

What 3D printing can already do三維打印已經做到的事

The technology is not a promise about the future. It is already being used at a scale that matters.這項技術不是對未來的許諾，它已經在實際規模上被使用。

In 2021, artificial coral reefs near Lantau Airport were created using 3D printing technology, and have been shown to protect the marine ecosystem.2021 年，機場附近水域以三維打印技術製成人工珊瑚礁，並已證實有助保護海洋生態。

StartmeupHK, 2023 — “3D-printed coral reefs: reviving marine ecosystems”StartmeupHK，2023——《3D打印環保珊瑚礁 以創科活化海洋生態圈》

It is a useful reminder that the same layer-by-layer method can rebuild a habitat as easily as it can make a part. After several years of development the technology has matured, which is why we think now is the right time to link 3D printing with environmental conservation.這提醒我們：同一種逐層建構的方法，可以用來重建一個棲息地，也可以只用來做一個零件。經過多年發展，技術已經成熟，所以我們認為現在正是把三維打印與環境保育連起來的時候。

Trade shows and partnerships展覽與合作

We are a small organisation, so being visible in the right room matters more than advertising.我們規模小，所以在對的場合露面，比賣廣告更重要。

Exhibiting at a trade show builds trust and a professional image: samples and certifications can be shown, and clients can meet the team face to face. It is also where partnerships begin — with local distributors, or with university research teams.參展可以建立信任與專業形象：展示樣本與認證，讓客戶親身認識團隊。合作也往往由這裡開始——與本地分銷商，或與大學研究團隊。

  • Samples, certifications and printed parts that people can hold可以親手拿起的樣本、認證與打印成品
  • Partnerships with distributors and university research teams與分銷商及大學研究團隊建立合作
  • Media coverage, especially for environmental and healthcare topics媒體報道，尤其是環保與醫療相關題材
  • Access to corporate marketing funds, which support the next programme申請企業市場推廣基金，支持下一個計劃

The road ahead前路

Three steps, in order. We do not skip the first one.三步，依次而行。我們不會跳過第一步。

  1. 01

    Establish in Hong Kong立足香港

    Build a solid base in the local market: understand 3D printing deeply, analyse market demand and test business models.在本土市場建立穩固基礎：深入理解三維打印，分析市場需求，測試商業模式。

  2. 02

    Make it repeatable變成可複製

    Turn the model into a repeatable system, then reproduce it in cities where the conditions allow, extending our influence beyond Hong Kong.把模式變成可複製的系統，然後在條件合適的城市重現，把影響力延伸到香港以外。

  3. 03

    Become a platform成為平台

    Build a business ecosystem and run it as a platform, so more people who need 3D printing can buy what they need at lower prices.建立商業生態並以平台形式運作，讓更多需要三維打印的人可以用更低價錢買到所需。

Where this fits the Sustainable Development Goals與可持續發展目標的呼應

Our work touches several of the UN Sustainable Development Goals. We describe this as alignment, not certification — we are a student project, not an accredited programme.我們的工作與聯合國多項可持續發展目標相關。我們稱之為「呼應」，而不是「認證」——我們是學生專案，不是受認可的計劃。

SDG 4 — Quality educationSDG 4——優質教育 SDG 3 — Good healthSDG 3——良好健康與福祉 SDG 11 — Sustainable citiesSDG 11——可持續城市 SDG 12 — Responsible consumption and productionSDG 12——負責任消費與生產

3D printing can print more than an object. It can print a more inclusive and compassionate society.三維打印打印的不只是一件物件，更是一個更共融、更有人情味的社會。
