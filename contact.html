Frequently asked questions常見問題

Is the recycled plastic safe to print?回收塑膠可以安全打印嗎？

We accept only clean PET, PP and HDPE (recycling codes 1, 5 and 2). Mixed, dirty or unidentified plastic is rejected at sorting. Prints are not used for food contact, and anything used in a clinical setting is approved by the partner hospital’s own safety process.我們只接受潔淨的 PET、PP 及 HDPE（回收編號 1、5、2）。混合、污穢或無法辨識的塑膠會在分類階段被剔除。打印成品不會用於接觸食物；任何臨床用途的物件，都須經合作醫院本身的安全程序批准。

Do participants need any experience?參加者需要有經驗嗎？

No. Workshops start with sketching on paper and a short explanation of how a printer works. Most participants print their first object within the session.不需要。工作坊由紙上草圖開始，並簡單講解打印機的運作。大部分參加者都能在同一節工作坊內打印出自己的第一件作品。

What does a workshop cost?工作坊收費如何？

Community and school workshops are free or charged at cost, because the material comes from plastic we have collected. A custom programme with a venue, materials and staff time is quoted individually.社區及學校工作坊免費或只收回成本，因為材料來自我們收集的塑膠。如屬度身訂造的計劃，涉及場地、材料與人手，則會另行報價。

Can we donate plastic?可以捐贈塑膠嗎？

Yes, if it is clean and sorted: empty bottles with caps and labels removed, codes 1, 5 and 2 only. Businesses with larger volumes should email us first so we can arrange collection.可以，只要是潔淨並已分類：空的膠樽，已移除瓶蓋與標籤，只限編號 1、5、2。如屬較大量的商業捐贈，請先電郵聯絡，以便安排收集。

Do you make medical devices?你們會製作醫療器材嗎？

Not on our own. Medical work is done with the partner hospital’s clinical team, in line with its regulatory, material and sterilisation requirements. Student prototypes are never used in patient care.我們不會自行製作。醫療項目由合作醫院的臨床團隊參與，並依從其法規、材料及消毒要求。學生原型不會用於病人護理。
