How it works運作方式

Technology and the 3R loop技術與 3R 循環

Recycled plastic is not a lesser material. Sorted, cleaned and processed properly it prints well — and it keeps waste out of landfill.回收塑膠不是次一等的材料。只要分類、清洗、加工得宜，它一樣打印得好——同時讓廢物不必送去堆填區。

What 3D printing actually is三維打印到底是什麼

Additive manufacturing增材製造

It is a process in which successive layers of material are laid down under computer control to make a three-dimensional object. Nothing is cut away, so there is very little offcut waste — the object is built only where it is needed.這是一個在電腦控制下，逐層鋪上材料以製成立體物件的過程。整個過程沒有切削，因此幾乎沒有邊角廢料——物件只在需要的位置生成。

That makes it good for one-off parts, small batches and repairs. A grip for a walking stick, a replacement clip for a hospital trolley, a model of a heart for a biology lesson: these are cheap to print and expensive to tool up for.這使它適合單件製作、小批量生產和維修。拐杖握把、醫院手推車的替換扣件、生物課用的心臟模型——這些東西打印起來便宜，但要開模生產就很貴。

There are limits, and we would rather name them. Prints are slow compared with injection moulding, the choice of materials is narrower, and the strength of a part depends on how it is oriented on the bed. We design around those limits instead of pretending they do not exist.它也有局限，我們寧願說清楚：打印速度比注塑慢，可選材料較少，而零件的強度取決於它在打印床上的放置方向。我們按這些局限來設計，而不是假裝它們不存在。

The loop, step by step循環，一步一步

Six steps from a used bottle to a finished object. Every one of them happens in Hong Kong.由一個用過的膠樽到一件完成品，共六個步驟，全部在香港發生。

  1. 01

    Collect收集

    Community points, housing estates and hospital collection bins take in clean PET, PP and HDPE bottles.社區收集點、屋苑及醫院回收箱，接收潔淨的 PET、PP、HDPE 膠樽。

  2. 02

    Sort分類

    Volunteers read the recycling code and reject anything mixed, dirty or unknown. Sorting is the step that decides quality.義工查看回收編號，剔除混合、污穢或來歷不明的塑膠。分類是決定質素的關鍵一步。

  3. 03

    Shred粉碎

    Bottles are stripped of caps and labels, washed, dried and shredded into flakes.膠樽去除瓶蓋與標籤，清洗、烘乾，再粉碎成薄片。

  4. 04

    Extrude製線

    Flakes are heated and drawn into filament of a consistent diameter, then spooled for the printer.薄片經加熱拉製成直徑一致的線材，再捲上線軸供打印機使用。

  5. 05

    Print打印

    The printer lays the filament down layer by layer, following a design made in a workshop or a classroom.打印機依工作坊或課堂完成的設計，逐層鋪上線材。

  6. 06

    Return回用

    Finished objects go to a student, a resident or a patient. Failed prints and supports go back into the shredder.完成品送到學生、居民或病人手上；打印失敗的成品與支撐材料，會送回粉碎步驟。

The three Rs, in practice三個 R，實際怎樣做

Reduce, reuse and recycle are not slogans here. Each one describes a decision we make at a different point in a product’s life.在這裡，減用、重用、回收不是口號。每一個都代表我們在產品生命週期不同階段所作的決定。

Reduce減用

We reduce the consumption of natural resources from the very beginning — at the design and material-selection stage. Printing only where material is needed means less waste than cutting a part out of a block.我們從一開始就減少天然資源的消耗——在設計與選材階段。只在需要的位置打印，比從一整塊材料切削出零件浪費更少。

Reuse重用

3D printing extends the life of products. A worn grip or a broken bracket can be printed again, so a device is repaired instead of being thrown away and replaced.三維打印延長產品壽命。磨損的握把或斷裂的支架可以重新打印，器具因此得以維修，而不是丟棄再買。

Recycle回收

Printing material can be made from waste plastic, or combined with it. Our PET, PP and HDPE collections feed directly into the filament we print with.打印材料可以由廢塑膠製成，或與廢塑膠混合。我們收集的 PET、PP、HDPE，直接成為打印線材的原料。

Safety and honest limits安全與誠實的局限

Recycled material is not the same as virgin material every time. We test each batch and we tell partners what they are getting.回收材料每次都不等於全新材料。我們會測試每一批材料，並如實告訴夥伴他們拿到的是什麼。

  • Material traceability: every spool is labelled with the plastic type, the batch and the date it was processed.材料可追溯：每一軸線材都標明塑膠種類、批次與加工日期。
  • No food contact and no body contact unless the partner’s own safety process approves it.不用於接觸食物或用於身體接觸，除非合作夥伴本身的安全程序批准。
  • Ventilation and filtration when printing, because heating plastic releases fumes.打印時保持通風並使用過濾，因為加熱塑膠會釋放氣體。
  • Medical devices follow the hospital’s regulatory and sterilisation requirements, not our preferences.醫療器材依從醫院的法規與消毒要求，而不是我們的偏好。
